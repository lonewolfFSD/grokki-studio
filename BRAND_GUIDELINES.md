# Grokki Studio Brand Guidelines

**October 2026**

## 1. Purpose

These guidelines establish rules for using the Grokki Studio name, logo, visual identity, and official brand assets.

Grokki Studio is an independent personal AI desktop assistant project. These guidelines aim to protect its identity, prevent confusion about official releases, and ensure that community projects can be distinguished from the original software.

## 2. Ownership of Brand Assets

The Grokki Studio name, official logos, application icons, original brand illustrations, promotional artwork, and other designated brand materials are reserved to their respective copyright or trademark owners.

Unless expressly stated otherwise, the software license accompanying the Grokki Studio source code does not grant permission to use its branding or official visual assets.

These guidelines do not claim ownership of third-party materials or grant rights that belong to others.

## 3. Official Name and Identity

The official project name is **Grokki Studio**.

You may refer to Grokki Studio when accurately describing the original project, discussing it, reviewing it, or explaining that your project is compatible with it.

Examples of acceptable descriptive use include:

- "Built using Grokki Studio."
- "A community modification of Grokki Studio."
- "Compatible with Grokki Studio."

Such references must be truthful and must not imply an affiliation that does not exist.

## 4. Logo and Official Visual Assets

Without prior written permission from the relevant rights holder, you may not use Grokki Studio's official logo, application icon, or designated brand artwork in a manner that:

- Implies that your product is an official Grokki Studio release.
- Suggests endorsement, sponsorship, partnership, or authorization that does not exist.
- Misleads users about the origin, developer, or support status of your software.
- Presents a modified or independently distributed product as the original official application.

Where permission has not been granted, forks and independently distributed derivatives should use their own names, logos, application icons, and visual identities.

## 5. Forks and Modified Versions

The Grokki Studio source code may be used, modified, or redistributed only as permitted by the applicable software license.

A person distributing a modified version must not falsely represent that version as the official Grokki Studio release.

Where required by the applicable software license or by law, distributors must preserve copyright notices, license texts, and other required attributions.

Modified versions should clearly identify their own project name and developer and should not use official branding in a misleading way.

These brand guidelines do not independently grant permission to copy, modify, or distribute the source code.

## 6. Commercial Use of Branding

Use of Grokki Studio branding in commercial products, paid services, advertising, merchandise, or other commercial activities requires prior written permission from the relevant rights holder, unless the particular use is otherwise authorized by applicable law.

Permission to use the software under its software license does not automatically constitute permission to use the Grokki Studio brand.

Commercial use of the software itself remains governed by the applicable software license.

## 7. Community Projects and Contributions

Community projects, plugins, integrations, themes, extensions, and tutorials may refer to Grokki Studio to describe compatibility or association accurately.

Community developers are encouraged to:

- Use their own project names and visual identities.
- Clearly distinguish independent projects from official releases.
- Avoid misleading claims of endorsement or affiliation.
- Respect the applicable software, asset, and third-party licenses.

Nothing in these guidelines prohibits truthful references, legitimate commentary, or other uses permitted by applicable law.

## 8. Third-Party Assets

Grokki Studio may incorporate third-party software, fonts, icons, illustrations, sounds, models, or other materials.

Such materials remain subject to their respective licenses and terms. These guidelines do not override those terms or transfer ownership of third-party intellectual property to Grokki Studio.

Users and distributors are responsible for complying with applicable third-party requirements.

## 9. Requests for Permission

Requests to use official Grokki Studio branding outside the permissions described in these guidelines should be submitted to the project maintainer through an official contact channel published by the project.

Requests should describe the intended use, the assets involved, and whether the use is commercial.

Permission must be obtained in writing before relying on it.

## 10. Enforcement and Updates

The relevant rights holder reserves the right to address unauthorized or misleading use of protected brand assets through appropriate means permitted by applicable law.

These guidelines may be updated as the project develops. Updates do not automatically change the terms of the software license governing a particular copy of the source code.

## 11. No Transfer of Ownership

These guidelines do not transfer ownership of the Grokki Studio name, logos, original artwork, or other protected brand materials.

All rights not expressly granted remain with their respective rights holders, subject to applicable law.

## 12. Contact

For branding permissions, questions, or reports of misleading use, contact the Grokki Studio maintainer through the official contact information published in the project repository.
